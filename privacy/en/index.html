Hosting on GitHub Pages

The legal basis is my legitimate interest in providing the website securely and free of charge (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR). For details, see the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Hosting on my own server

Other websites are served from my own server, which I rent from Hetzner Online GmbH, Industriestr. 25, 91710 Gunzenhausen, Germany, in a data center in Germany. Hetzner processes the data on my behalf (Art. 28 GDPR).

The web server processes your IP address to deliver the page. No access logs are written. Technical error messages are kept for no more than 14 days. The legal basis is my legitimate interest in providing the website securely (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR).

Visitor statistics

Websites that refer to this section measure their reach with self-hosted analytics software on my own server (see above). The data is not passed on to third parties and is not processed outside the EU.

Currently used: Umami, self-hosted

What is recorded: the page visited (address and title), the referring page, browser, operating system, device type, screen size, language, time, and country, region and city, roughly estimated from the IP address.

What does not happen: No cookies are set and nothing is stored on your device. Your IP address is not stored. It is used only briefly to estimate your approximate location and to form a pseudonymous identifier that groups page views from the same browser within a calendar month. There is no session recording and no profile across websites.

Legal basis: my legitimate interest in simple reach measurement (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR).

Retention: The statistics are kept without a fixed time limit for long-term comparisons. They contain no IP addresses and usually cannot be linked to a person. In encrypted backups with a storage provider whose data center is in the EU, they may remain for up to 12 more months after deletion.

Objection: You can object to the recording at any time, most easily by blocking the script from trk.srvkist.net with a content blocker.

Contact by email

If you write to me, I process your message and your address to reply (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR). I delete them once the matter is settled.

Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure and restriction of processing (Art. 15 to 18 GDPR) and to data portability (Art. 20 GDPR), and you can object to processing based on legitimate interest (Art. 21 GDPR). To do so, write to me at simbo@simbo.de. You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, for example the State Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information Baden-Württemberg.

Because the statistics do not store IP addresses, I usually cannot link page views to you and therefore cannot provide access to or delete them individually (Art. 11 GDPR).
